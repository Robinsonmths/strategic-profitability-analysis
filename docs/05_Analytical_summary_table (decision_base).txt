STEP 5 — ANALYTICAL SUMMARY TABLE (DECISION BASE)

Step Objective

Consolidate all validated metrics from previous steps into a single decision table that allows to:

- Compare categories, products, and markets objectively
- Identify where profit is generated, eroded, and destroyed
- Serve as a direct basis for strategic classification (Prioritize / Adjust / Eliminate)

At this stage, there is no visualization, only consolidated and coherent numbers.

Unit of Analysis

Analysis will be performed at three levels, always independently:

- Category
- Product
- Market / Region

Each level generates its own summary table.

Summary Table Structure

Each table should contain, at minimum, the following columns:

Identification

- Category
- Product (when applicable)
- Market / Region (when applicable)

Volume and Revenue

- Total Revenue (Sales)
- Total Quantity Sold (Quantity)

Costs and Results

- Total Shipping Cost
- Gross Profit (Profit)
- Real Profit (Profit − Shipping Cost)

Derived Metrics

- Real Margin (%)
  Real Profit / Total Revenue
- Volume-weighted Average Discount
- Shipping Cost as % of Revenue

Business Impact

- % Contribution to Total Profit
- % Contribution to Total Loss (when applicable)

Important Rules

- No new metrics are created here
  Only consolidation of what was already defined in Step 3.
- No raw data is altered
- Invalid rows should already have been treated or flagged in Step 4
- All metrics must be financially coherent

Table Reading Criteria

The table should allow answering, just by looking at the numbers:

- Who sells a lot but generates little profit?
- Who generates absolute loss?
- Where does shipping consume the results?
- Where is the discount above the return generated?

If the answer requires “creative interpretation,” the table is wrong.

Expected Output of Step 5

At the end of this step, you should have:

- 1 summary table by Category
- 1 summary table by Product
- 1 summary table by Market / Region

All purely numerical, with no classification yet.
