Executive Briefing & Decision Map

1. Am I making money the right way?
Revenue is growing — but is profit growing at the same pace?
Which categories generate high sales volume but fail to convert into cash flow?
Where should sales efforts be reduced or stopped?

2. Where exactly am I losing money?
Which products generate direct losses?
What is the root cause?
Pricing?
Discount strategy?
Shipping cost?
Am I effectively paying to sell?

3. Is discounting helping or destroying the business?
What is the healthy limit for discounts?
Are there products that only sell with high discounts?
If discounts are reduced by X%, does profit improve or does volume collapse?

4. Where should I focus my strategy?
Which markets or regions are worth scaling?
Where should growth be limited or exited?
Where does commercial effort generate the highest return per order?

5. Is the issue product-related or operational?
Is shipping cost eroding profitability?
Are there regions where logistics costs are economically unjustifiable?
Should the company:
Increase prices?
Change shipping policy?
Reduce the product portfolio?

6. If I had to make ONE decision today…
“What would you change tomorrow if you were in my position?”
Analytical Structure

Stage 1 — Business Question
“Which products and categories should be prioritized, adjusted, or eliminated to improve profitability without relying on revenue growth?”

Stage 2 — Hypotheses
H1: Categories with higher average discounts present significantly lower margins.
H2: A small subset of categories concentrates most of the total losses.
H3: In some categories, shipping cost is the primary driver of profit erosion.

Stage 3 — Metrics Definition
Which metrics answer these questions?
Percentages and margins
Volume and contribution percentage
Comparison of results with and without shipping cost

Stage 4 — Data Quality Gate
Identification of invalid values
Investigation of external or abnormal factors
Financial logic validation

Stage 5 — Analytical Summary Tables
Category-level aggregation
Product-level aggregation
Market / Region-level aggregation
At this stage, the analysis is strictly numerical.
No classification or decision-making is performed yet.

Stage 6 — Scenario Exploration & Decision
Scenario simulation
Decision filtering
Executive conclusions and recommendations
Category Classification Framework (Final)

🟢 PRIORITIZE
Positive real profit
Healthy margins
Low dependency on discounts
Shipping cost does not compromise results

🟡 ADJUST
Positive profit with low margins
OR
Negative profit with high sales volume
OR
High sensitivity to discounts or shipping cost
Possible actions:
Repricing
Discount strategy adjustment
Logistics review

🔴 ELIMINATE
Negative real profit
Recurring negative margins
Logistics makes the category economically unviable
Action: discontinue or renegotiate.
